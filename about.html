About

Building stylized worlds with production precision

Bio

I am a worldbuilder focused on high-fidelity voxel aesthetics for Minecraft and Hytale ecosystems. My work blends design intent, gameplay readability, and visual storytelling.

Tools Used

  • Minecraft creative and command workflows
  • Hytale block and entity prototyping pipelines
  • Blockbench for character and creature variations
  • Aseprite and Photoshop for texture direction
  • Codex-assisted iteration for planning, scripting, and polish passes

Creative Process

  1. Concept brief, moodboard, and world tone alignment.
  2. Blockout pass for silhouette and navigation flow.
  3. Detailing phase with modular assets and biome consistency.
  4. Lighting, cinematic capture, and optimization for gameplay clarity.
  5. Review loops and variation passes guided by Codex-assisted notes.
